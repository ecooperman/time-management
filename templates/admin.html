People

Tag jobs by who they are for (e.g. yourself and a partner whose job search you track here). Purely a label for filtering the Jobs page: it is not an account and gives nobody access to anything - your jobs, tasks and reminders are private to you. Administrators can also pick which resume (managed in the resume app's own People page) each person's "Generate Resume + Cover Letter" tailoring starts from.

Reminders

Push notifications for tasks with a reminder set (see a task's edit form). Per-device - enable this on every phone/browser you want nagged, not just one. iOS needs 16.4+ and this app added to your home screen first (not a regular Safari tab).
