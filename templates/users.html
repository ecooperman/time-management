Users

Add someone

Lets them into Time: they can create an account (a passkey is quickest) and are emailed how. Only administrators can invite. Everyone's tasks, jobs and reminders are completely private to them, and you can't see anyone else's. Adding someone you removed restores them.

People with access

Removing someone only stops them using Time. What they own stays private and untouched until you delete it, and restoring them puts everything back.

Removed

These people can't sign in to Time. Deleting their data erases every task, job and category they own and can't be undone; their email stays here, removed.
